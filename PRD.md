# PRD — The Chronicles of Trust

## 1. Problem Statement
Many educational tools for ethics are dry and lack immersion. There is a need for a "vibe-coded" experience that gamifies complex ethical frameworks through historical context and high-fidelity visuals.

## 2. Target User
Students, lifelong learners, and business professionals interested in the evolution of ethics and corporate responsibility.

## 3. Core Features (v1)
- **Historical Narrative Loop**: 6 levels of ethical dilemmas.
- **Triple Bottom Line Balance**: Real-time feedback on E/S/G impacts.
- **Archivist Collection**: A repository of recovered ethical principles.
- **AI-Powered Immersion**: Dynamic visuals that change per era.
- **Persistence**: Save/Load system for continuous play.

## 4. Visual & UX Direction
- **Tone**: Cinematic, Mysterious, High-Tech but rooted in History.
- **Style**: Dark mode, Glassmorphism, Emerald/Blue accent colors.
- **Micro-interactions**: "Glitch" effects on transitions, smooth fades.

## 5. Success Metrics
- Player completes all 6 eras.
- High level of immersion (indicated by time spent in narrative).
- Successful retrieval of all 6 Ethical Shards.

## 6. Business Rules Hardcoded
- Principles, Values, and Norms (PVN) must be explicitly mentioned in historical contexts.
- Decisions cannot be "wrong" in a game-over sense but must show meaningful consequences.
